I found this album via [Lars Gotrich's top 10](https://www.npr.org/2025/12/11/g-s1-99860/best-albums-of-2025-lars-gotrich), I picked it out to listen without reading anything at all and was blown away to hear wonderful American Primitive songs.

If you don't know what [American Primitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_primitive_guitar) music is, the term was coined by John Fahey for his style of music; I recommend you go and read the wiki article for more.

Some of my other favorites in the genre are:

- [Death Chants, Breakdowns & Military Waltzes - John Fahey](https://rateyourmusic.com/release/album/john-fahey/vol-ii-death-chants-breakdowns-and-military-waltzes/)
- [6- & 12-String Guitar - Leo Kottke](https://rateyourmusic.com/release/album/leo-kottke/6-and-12-string-guitar/)
- [Kensington Blues - Jack Rose](https://rateyourmusic.com/release/album/jack-rose/kensington-blues/)
