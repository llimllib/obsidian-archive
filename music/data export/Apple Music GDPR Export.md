https://mystats.music/guides/apple-music-history

The above guide provides instructions for getting an Apple music history export, which I struggled to figure out without it:

1. https://privacy.apple.com/
2. `Get a copy of your data`
3. select `Apple media services`
4. `Continue`
5. `Complete Request`
6. Wait until they email you about the export
