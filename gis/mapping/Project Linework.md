https://www.projectlinework.org/

> A library of handcrafted vector linework for cartography, each designed in a unique aesthetic style. They are meant to break us away from default line paths that we so often rely on by providing more visually-interesting alternatives.

> Linework sets are available in the following formats: [Shapefile](http://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Shapefiles), [GeoJSON](http://geojson.org/), and [Adobe Illustrator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobe_Illustrator_Artwork) (PDF & Inkscape compatible)
