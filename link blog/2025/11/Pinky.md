> Pinky is an extremely simple scripting language that is mostly used by beginners when writing their first compiler implementation.

> Pinky is the perfect _toy_ language for those who are just starting their journey into learning about compilers & interpreters. The language is ridiculously small, allowing students to really focus on _compiler concepts_ instead of hipster language features.

> Think of a procedural language with a tiny Lua-like [syntax](https://pinky-lang.org/syntax.html).
