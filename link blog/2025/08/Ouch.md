https://github.com/ouch-org/ouch
https://crates.io/crates/ouch

A tool to improve and unify the UI of various compression programs.

`ouch decompress <file>` will attempt to discover the filetype of file and decompress it, no matter what format it's in, for a large variety of formats.

`ouch compress [<file>...] <archive>` will compress files, and guess the format you want from the name of the archive you provide.
