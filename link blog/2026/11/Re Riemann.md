> And I see this mural, and think: how great is Man! Our life is like a breath of air, we were beset by illness and hunger and cold, millions and hundreds of millions died in plagues. And yet we—sick, hungry, short-lived—learned the system of the world, crawled out of the caves, brought nature to heel, walked on the Moon. The last shall be first! And eyes that are clouded with age have seen the furthest stars, and the lives of a cell, and the worlds of Cantor and Hilbert: the invisible world that waited forever. We are dust and ashes, and yet our minds bestride the universe. How great is Man!

> And when I read the Riemann announcement, when I read [_Machines of Loving Grace_](https://darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace): humanity is not at the center. Claude is at the center. We are hidden in the margins, passive recipients of manna from Claude. How great is Claude! How noble in reason, how infinite in benevolence! The dancing star, the living [word](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%BB%CF%8C%CE%B3%CE%BF%CF%82#Ancient_Greek)!

- [Fernando Borretti](https://borretti.me/article/re-riemann)
