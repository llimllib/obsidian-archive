- install process:
	- download app
	- download [required files](https://github.com/K3V1991/Xbox-Emulator-Files/releases) and unzip them somewhere
	- open the app
	- add the required files from the location you unzipped them to (I used `~/.config/share/xbox`)
	- download a ROM (I used [Ninja Gaiden black](https://archive.org/download/ninja-gaiden-black))
	- click "load disc" and point it at your ROM
