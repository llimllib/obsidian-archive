https://arhan.sh/blog/native-instant-space-switching-on-macos/

It's driven me crazy for a long time that space switching on mac is so slow; enough that I had given up all hope of getting it faster. That article points to an app I'd not seen before:

https://github.com/jurplel/InstantSpaceSwitcher

I've installed the app, and it works with its own shortcut, but the mac default shortcut overrides it. To disable ctrl+right and left for switching, I went to `system preferences -> keyboard -> keyboard shorcuts... -> Mission Control -> Mission Control (command group)` and disabled the `Move left a space` and `Move right a space` command shortcuts.

With that, instant (approximately) switching is mine after so long!

The [news.yc comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47708818) point out that [[Aerospace]], which I used for a little while, also provides this and suggest that [[OmniWM]] solves this too.
